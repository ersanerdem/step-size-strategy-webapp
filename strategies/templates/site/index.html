{% translate "Euler's method with a variable step size" %}

{% translate "Euler's method approximates the solution of $x' = f(t,x)$ step by step: $x_{i+1} = x_i + h_i f(t_i, x_i)$. With a fixed step $h$, a step small enough for the steep parts of the solution wastes work where the solution is flat, and a large step loses accuracy where the solution changes quickly." %}

{% translate "A step size strategy chooses $h_i$ anew in every step from bounds of the problem: so that the next point stays where Picard's theorem guarantees a unique solution, or so that the local error stays below a chosen level $\delta$. Here you can try these strategies on your own equations and on classical engineering models, and watch the step size adapt." %}
